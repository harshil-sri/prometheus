## 2. The 10 Inviolable Design Laws

1. **No fabrication.** No hardcoded metric strings, no fake evidence, no placeholder
   numbers in UI/reports/docs. Report builders accept typed `ComputedEvidence`
   objects only — a raw string-literal evidence dict must raise.
2. **Two holdout axes.** Held-out attack TYPES (A2, A5) AND held-out generation
   MECHANISMS are locked + hashed before training; leakage asserts cover both.
3. **No train-on-eval.** Beat-2 improvement is measured on FROZEN eval plans
   (fixed fingerprints, fresh seeds) never present in retraining data.
4. **Fast path = pure ML** (XGB+GNN+meta, <100 ms). Structured 0–1000 score is
   deep-path only. Never mixed (two of its inputs don't exist at fast-path time).
5. **Determinism.** Every component seeded; same seed ⇒ byte-identical artifacts.
6. **External queries with synthetic names only** (sanctions/OSINT services hit
   real infrastructure; never send anything that could resolve to a real person).
7. **Evidence-ID provenance.** Every claim in investigator output / docx traces to
   an `evidence_id` in the EvidenceStore. "No report without validation."
8. **Version registry.** Every blue-model version and red strategy is registered;
   final "exploitability" = worst-case recall across the whole attack population.
9. **Pre-registered kill criteria.** Stretch modules (RL, TDA) ship only if their
   kill criterion (committed BEFORE building) is met; otherwise the honest
   negative result ships as a rigor artifact.
10. **Orchestrator never executes.** CaseManager delegates all work (Strix
    pattern); it never fetches OSINT / queries graphs itself. Global call budget
    enforced.
